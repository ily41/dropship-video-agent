## What it is (1-2 sentences, plain words)
A bag shaped like a realistic white fluffy Samoyed dog. People wear it on the back or the front.
It opens in two halves with a zipper around the middle, and people keep things like makeup and a phone inside.

- upright pointed ears, glossy black round eyes, black nose, open smiling mouth with pink tongue
- two black leather straps attached at the lower sides with gold metal clasps
- gold zipper along the side seam; inside is lined with brown suede-like fabric
- only one colour: white
